# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versioning is semantic: the major version changes when the meaning of a defined term or the shape of the hierarchy changes, the minor version when substantive content is added, and the patch version for corrections that do not change meaning.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-03

### Concept foundation

- Published the founding paper: the concept of the Agentic Operating Procedure, its origin in procedural engineering, and its structural difference from a Standard Operating Procedure.
- Published the six-level conceptual hierarchy with the architectural responsibility carried by each level, and the governing principle that the procedure governs the agent.
- Published the glossary with a stable identifier for every defined term.
- Published the non-goals and the misconception ladder.
